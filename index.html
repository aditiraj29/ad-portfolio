Video Outreach ships in Vini AI — twelve campaigns across sales and service

By Aditi Raj · Product Management Intern

Campaigns can now send a short, personal video from a sales or service agent along with every text — personalized with the customer’s name and vehicle.

I recently launched this at Spyne: Video Outreach, a native step in Vini AI’s sales and service campaigns. Pick a use case, add it to a campaign, and every follow-up goes out with a short personal video from an agent.

It ships as twelve ready-to-use campaigns — nine for sales, three for service — each with its own video and script, in male and female versions.
